# Prototype A': the current builder with an honest objective

Written 2026-11-08 for the builder review (`docs/algo-review.md`, Phase 2). A' is the shortlist's first prototype (`docs/review/shortlist.md`, "The final list"): the current builder's own code with an honest supply-and-demand objective, in two versions.

| Version       | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `a-objective` | A copy of `packages/core/src/builder.ts` (26.0.35) whose themed styles count setup and payoff once, by `theme-rules.json`, with one plan-health term and a payoff gate. The copy's swap search. |
| `a-search`    | The same builder with a different search: lexicographic comparison, neighbourhoods by mana value and job, a package move, tabu memory, and fixed caps.                                          |

Code: `packages/pipeline/src/review/algos/a/` (`builder.ts`, `theme.ts`, `search.ts`, `select.ts`, `version.ts`, `a-objective.ts`, `a-search.ts`). Tests: `packages/pipeline/test/review-proto-a.test.ts`. The core, `config/`, and the learned data are unchanged.

**Held-out pools.** A' was developed and tuned only against `plain-01` to `plain-10` and the public pools (`data/review/pools/public/`). It never built, and its author never looked at, `plain-11` to `plain-30` or the theme pools; those are held out for judging.

## Design

The review found that the current builder's failure is in what it counts (`docs/review/our-builder.md`, "Where a card can get in for a reason unrelated to how the deck plays"): setup and payoff are yes-or-no tag matches with no amount, a dual card counts on both sides everywhere, and the only balance term is the hand rules' min(enablers, payoffs) at a quarter weight. A' keeps the algorithm and changes only that, following the research's first three ideas (`docs/review/research.md`, "Ideas worth borrowing, ranked": count once with supply and demand, balance in the objective, a payoff gate in every layer), plus ideas 7 and 11 in `a-search` (plan health before strength; package moves and tabu).

For a style that `theme-rules.json` describes (all 18 themed styles in version 2 of the file):

1. **Count once, with amounts.** Each spell is setup with a supply weight, payoff with a demand weight, or neither, by `matchCard` in `theme-rules.ts`: the side whose matching rules reach the higher priority wins, then the higher weight, a tie going to payoff; an exclude rule removes the card from both. This is the same resolution `review:check` uses. The config matchers still give roles, curve, lands, and strength, but no longer theme membership.
2. **Balance in the objective.** One plan-health term replaces the hand rules' min(enablers, payoffs), the theme-hit bonus (worth 2 for a dual card), the learned plan fit, the learned theme target, and the drift cap on theme cards. The hand rules leave the shapes' objective too, so best fit compares shapes and themes on even terms.
3. **A payoff gate in every pass** that adds a card: role fills, the theme pass, color minimums, type minimums, the curve pass, filler, the swap search, the package move, and the land refit's refills.
4. **Viability and plan choice by honest counts.** A requested style is built only when the pool, in the deck's colors, holds the rules' floors for the size, counted once; otherwise the build throws `BuildError`. Best fit builds every style the honest scan calls viable plus every viable shape, and keeps the healthiest plan.

Everything else runs as the copy did, apart from one guard that keeps each color of a multi-color deck at its minimum through the swaps (below): roles and role credit for the commander, curve, type mix, lands and the land refit, colors and cast penalties, the mana base, Commander identity and tribes, learned pairs and combos, the commander's EDHREC card fit, card strength, and the deck score.

Tribes (`tribal:<Type>`) and secondary styles are not in the rules file, so they count with the current matchers, as in the copy. A style the rules do not describe would too; with `EXAMPLE_THEME_RULES` (the fallback when `theme-rules.json` is missing, with a loud warning) that is every style but Graveyard.

## Plan health

For a style and size, `planSpec` (`theme.ts`) reads the rules' minimums. The floors are the alternatives the rules give (for example Counters at 60: setup 10, repeatable 4, payoff 5; or setup 13, payoff 5). The first alternative sets:

- setup need _S_ = its `setupWeight`, else its `setup`;
- payoff need _P_ = its `payoffWeight`, else its `payoff`;
- the ratio _r_ = _S_ / _P_, or 1 when the rules give no payoff floor (Evasion, Burn, Mill at 20);
- the scale _N_ = the sum of its floors.

For a deck with weighted setup supply _s_ (copies times weight) and weighted payoff demand _d_:

- **progress** = for the alternative closest to met, the sum over its floors of min(have, need), over the sum of the needs. It is 1 once every floor of an alternative is met and cannot rise further.
- **short** = max(0, _r_ × _d_ − _s_).
- **lone** = payoff copies the supply cannot feed, feeding the cheapest payoffs first at _r_ times their weight.

The objective's plan term (`planTerm`) is 2 × _N_ × progress − 2 × short − 2 × lone. Each floor unit is worth 2 objective points, as much as a copy toward a role target (`roleShortfall` 2), and nothing once the floors are met, so theme density past the minimums stops paying. Card strength is worth 0.3 per point, so below the floors one setup unit outweighs about 6.7 strength points.

Plan health on one scale for every style (`planHealth`, used by best fit) is progress − short / max(1, _r_ × max(_d_, _P_)) − lone / max(1, payoff copies), at most 1. A shape scores 1, and a tribe or a style without rules scores its current matchers' minimums met (the lower of enablers over their minimum and payoffs over theirs, capped at 1). Every deck's health is then capped by its cover of role minimums (the mean of min(1, have / min) over roles with a minimum).

## The gate

`gateOpen` (`theme.ts`): a payoff of weight _w_ may join only when _s_ ≥ _r_ × (_d_ + _w_), where _s_ and _d_ count every card already in the deck (and the commander). A card is setup or payoff, never both, so a payoff adds nothing to _s_ and never meets its own need. Setup and cards with no job are never held back. The gate applies in `pickBest` (every fill pass), in both searches against the deck without the outgoing card, and in the package move's refill. A trim from the land refit can still take setup out from under a payoff, so `repairBalance` runs after the last refit. The mana base, chosen after the spells by the core's `chooseManaBase`, is gated too: when the nonbasic lands it picked bring a payoff the deck's setup cannot feed (threshold lands such as Cabal Pit and Cephalid Coliseum, Memorial to Folly, or Gaea's Cradle), it is chosen again without payoff lands. Before the reviewer added that step, `review:check`'s count, which reads lands, found an unfed payoff in 16 of a-objective's 732 themed development decks and 15 of a-search's 687, every one from such a land; after it, none of those 31 jobs has one, and the ten decks in `data/review/decks/a-*` did not change.

## The a-search changes

`search.ts`, `lexiSearch`, in place of `localSearch`:

- **Lexicographic comparison.** `compareTiers`: plan tier first, then shape (curve, type mix, roles), then value (strength, cast penalties, learned pairs, combos, commander fit, learned role and type drift). Legality is a filter, not a tier: every move keeps the copy limits, type caps, the gate, and each color's minimum. No strength gain can buy a setup card's place or a role copy.
- **Neighbourhoods.** For each outgoing card, the candidates with the same mana value bucket or the same job (the same theme side, or a shared role) are tried first, then the rest, each in id order. The first improvement is taken, and the next scan starts after the card just moved, as the copy's passes go round the deck.
- **Package move.** When no single swap improves, take out one payoff with the setup only it needed (setup worth least first, up to _r_ times the payoff's weight, each removal keeping the floors met and the balance) and refill through the gated passes with those cards held out. Tried on the two weakest payoffs; kept only when the deck compares better.
- **Tabu memory.** The last 8 cards swapped in or out may not move. When neither a swap nor a package improves, up to 2 sideways swaps (nearby candidates only, plan and shape no worse, the least value lost) let the search cross a dip; the best deck seen is restored at the end.
- **Fixed caps** (`SEARCH_CAPS`): 200 moves, 10,000 candidate decks scored per build attempt, 2 package tries per stall, 2 sideways moves. No clock is read and nothing is random; the seed is ignored. The first caps (25,000 scored decks, 3 and 3) cost 2 to 3.5 times head, almost all of it in full rounds of single swaps that found nothing after a sideways move; at 10,000 the search costs about what the copy's does.

## Known weaknesses

1. **Best fit leans to the plain shapes.** Once a theme meets its floors its health is 1, the same as a shape's, so the copy's deck score decides, and that score rates shapes at or above themes (on `bulk-red` R 60 head's own Midrange scores 86.8, Aggro 86.2, Artifacts 85.6; head avoids the lean only because its best fit ranks by learned plan scores, which A' does not read). On the 161 best-fit jobs at 60 in the development sample, a-objective built a shape 83 times (Aggro 54), against 50 for head and 43 for `head-web`, which ranks by the same deck score; a-search, whose strength tier polishes shape decks further, built a shape 122 times (Aggro 69, Midrange 44). Plan health says which plans are broken, not which whole plan is better. Plan choice is the shortlist's next-phase item, and best-fit results for A' should be read with this in mind: a decent rate there partly measures plain decks. Reviewer's check (`data/review/scratch/rv-lean.mjs`, `rv-parts.ts`): when a shape won at 60, a theme at health 1 trailed it by a median 1.2 points (a-objective; 1.5 for a-search), and rebuilding 30 such pairs as styled requests, the shape's cards were stronger in 24 and its closeness higher in 19, so the lean mostly reflects themed decks at their floors carrying weaker cards, which the shortlist's "a themed deck should have to beat the plain deck" accepts; a tie-break for a met theme whose cards are no weaker would flip about 1 in 5. The lean is not one way at every size: at 40 a shape won only 8 of 161 best fits (weakness 9), and at 100 Ramp won 14 of 38 (both `plain-03` commanders in the reviewer's timing run too), each from the same deck score. Read by the owner's rule that a skew toward one style is a regression, this would block shipping A' best fit as is; the plan picker is the place to fix it.
2. **Setup stops at the floor.** Past the minimums the plan term pays nothing, so the swap search trades extra setup for strength and themed decks settle at the rules' floors: the Blue Graveyard deck has 8 setup weight against the hand build's 14, and of a-objective's 182 styled 60-card decks in the development sample, the median holds exactly its setup floor and 145 sit within 15% of it. A deck at its floors shows its plan in most games by the guide's odds, but with nothing to spare. A small value for setup past the floor, until the ratio to the payoffs in the deck is comfortably met, is the obvious next change. It was left out because the research found filling past the minimums to be part of head's failure (`docs/review/research.md`, finding 2: "learned targets fill well past the minimums"), though there the extra cards were false friends the rules no longer count, and the guide sets each floor where the plan shows up by turn 5 in most games. Any such slope must sit in a-search's value tier, not its plan tier: in the plan tier every extra setup unit would outrank all card strength, and the search would pack the weakest setup the pool has.
3. **More declines.** A' declines what the honest scan rules out: on the development sample, 63 of 293 styled requests at 60, 78 of 320 at 40, 38 of 115 at 20, and 17 of 38 at 100 that head builds (most often Counters, Enchantments, Tappers, Lifegain, Energy, and Group hug). Head builds those decks, but only 65 of its 245 themed 60-card decks meet the honest minimums. Collation should compare decent rates both over built decks and over all requests.
4. **Lands count only at the gate.** The mana base picks lands after the spells, so fetch and bounce lands for Landfall and artifact lands for Artifacts count neither in the builder's tally nor in its viability scan, while `review:check` counts every card. A' is stricter than the rules there. The one place lands count is the mana base's own gate (see "The gate"): a payoff land comes in only when the setup feeds it.
5. **Type caps can hold a theme under its floor.** The fill keeps the copy's type caps, so a Voltron deck at 40 can stop at 6 of its 7 setup cards when the noncreature-permanent cap is full; 25 of the 732 honest a-objective decks in the sample (15 of 687 for a-search) missed a floor this way or by running out of cards.
6. **Only as good as the rules.** A' plays what the rules weigh. Four Otterball Antics and two Ureni's Rebuff (flashback, 0.5 each) fill most of the Blue Graveyard deck's payoff weight; the rules count them, so A' does.
7. **Tribes and secondaries use the current matchers.** The rules file has no tribes, so `tribal:Elf` under Lathril and every secondary style count as the copy does. Under a-search, a tribe's own pairs and its theme hits (one per member side, with no cap) sit in the plan tier, so its tribe decks can give up curve and role points to more members: in the development sample, a-search's `public-elf-tribe` Elf deck under Abomination of Llanowar fell short of the ramp, removal, sweeper, and finisher targets (minimums held) that a-objective's meets, scoring 80.2 to 83.6. The Lathril Elf deck still won best fit under both versions, on the commander's pull. A fix would cap the tribe's plan tier at its minimums, as `planTerm` does, and needs the copy's tribe guard in `lexiSearch`, which has none.
8. **Best fit costs more.** A' builds every viable style (7 to 14 on the larger public pools, more on the 3,000-card pools) where the copy built the top 3 themes and the shapes, so best fit takes longer than head's even though each build costs about the same.
9. **The deck score still reads the hand rules at 40.** The 40-card deck score weighs the hand rules' pairs at 0.6, though no A' objective chases them any more. It breaks best fit's ties on plan health, and nearly every style best fit builds reaches health 1, so at 40 the hand rules' dual-counted pairs largely pick the plan: themes win 153 of 161 best fits there, against shapes' 83 of 161 at 60, where the deck score is closeness to real decks instead.

## What A' does not do

- It changes nothing in `packages/core`, `config/`, the apps, or the learned data. The learned plan priors and targets still count with the current matchers; A' simply stops reading them for honest styles (no plan scores, no theme target, no plan fit, no theme caps) instead of relearning them from honest counts.
- No `requires` rules (research idea 6): a card whose condition the deck does not meet (Ajani's Comrade with no Ajani) is not checked.
- Lands never count as setup or payoff in the builder's tally, only at the mana base's gate (see the weaknesses).
- No color-aware weighting of theme cards (idea 12) beyond the copy's cast penalties; every request below 100 in the development set was mono-color or a two-color slice.
- Secondary styles and tribes are not counted by the rules.
- No two-for-two swaps; the package move only takes a payoff out with its setup and refills.
- No plan picker for the Build page's options (`head-web`); best fit is A's own single deck.
- No clock-based limits: the caps are counts, so a build is deterministic and costs about the same on every machine relative to head.
- It never built or read `plain-11` to `plain-30` or the theme pools.
