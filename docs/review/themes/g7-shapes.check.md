# Check: theme guide for the plain shapes (g7-shapes.md)

Checker's notes on `docs/review/themes/g7-shapes.md` (Aggro, Midrange, Control).

## What held up

- All three sections and the opening "Judging a plain deck" are complete.
- Every example card was found by `node packages/pipeline/src/review/cards.ts <names>` (outputs in `data/review/scratch/g7chk/aggro.txt`, `mid.txt`, `ctrl.txt`). Quoted oracle text, costs, power and toughness, rarity notes, and the live roles the guide names all match: Wall of Wood and Goblin Piker meet `earlyCreature`, Lava Axe and Lava Spike meet `removal`, Healing Salve and Deathgrip meet `cheapInteraction`, Pyroblast meets both at strength 9.37 (Lightning Bolt 7.89), Craw Wurm and Enormous Baloth meet `finisher`, Gravedigger and Cancel meet no role, Opt meets `cardAdvantage`.
- Template and profile claims match the config: aggro removal 3/5 and 2/4, control sweeper min 1 and `cardAdvantage` 4/7, no `100` overrides for aggro or control, 100-card removal 6/8 and card advantage 8/12. A composition style is not viable when any role minimum is unmet (`packages/core/src/scan.ts`, the `notes.length === 0` test). A primary style at 100 uses the Commander curve (`effectiveCurveProfile`), about 9 cards at six or more. The control cap of 0.5 gives about 17, 11, and 6 creatures. The land formula rounds 25 + 2.0 x (3.7 - 2.9) to 27 and 25 + 2.0 x (2.4 - 2.9) to 24 (`packages/core/src/lands.ts`).
- The writer's counts reproduce with `node data/review/scratch/g7-gaps.mjs`: 166, 58, 54, and sweepers W 7, U 8, B 88, R 100. The plan medians reproduce from `config/learned/plans.json`: aggro removal 7 and 5, midrange removal 7, early creatures 8.5, creatures 22.6, lands 25, Commander midrange removal 9.8 and card advantage 16.3, control creatures 21.6, 14.0, and 8.0, Commander control removal 9.0, sweepers 3.1, card advantage 14.2. Rule 903.7 says 40 life.
- The 20-card numbers double up cleanly into the 40-card numbers for all three shapes.

## Issues

- **Aggro.** Claim: "14 cheap attackers give a play on each of the first three turns in most games." Problem: the odds are much lower. With 14 attackers in 60 cards, the chance of at least one by turn 1, two by turn 2, and three by turn 3 on the play (7, 8, 9 cards seen) is 0.35. Even three attackers among 9 cards, ignoring cost, is 0.35. So the 14+ floor (and 10+ at 40) is too low for the stated reasoning. Evidence: `node data/review/scratch/g7chk/hyper.mjs` gives 0.35 for 14 of 60, 0.64 for 20 of 60, 0.85 for 26 of 60, and 0.40 for 10 of 40. Real Challenger aggro decks run 16 (Mono-Red Aggro) and 24 (Mono White Aggro) attackers by the guide's own definition (`node data/review/scratch/g7c-decks.mjs precon:MonoRedAggro_Q05 precon:MonoWhiteAggro_Q07`). Severity: **wrong**.

- **Control.** Claims: "as real control decks do (about 22 creatures at 60)", "real control decks play 22, 14, and 8", "about 4" cards at six or more, "25 to 26" lands, "real control Commander decks (43)". Problem: these are decks the learner labels `control`, not control decks. The label holds whatever matched no theme. The named control precons look nothing like 22 creatures. The proposals built on this evidence (raise the creature cap to 60%, cap lands at 26) would push Control toward Midrange. That risks the style-mix problem the owner treats as a blocker. Evidence: `node data/review/scratch/g7c-shapes.ts` lists the 27 decks behind the 60-card `control` profile: Draconic Fury, Blossoming Growth, Dino Might, Endless Hordes, Selesnya Conclave, Surprise Attack, Wild Rush (a green intro pack with 10 finishers and 14 cards at five or more). It labels Azorius Control (Q05) `enchantments` and Dimir Control (Q08) `graveyard`. It lists the 43 Commander "control" decks as Party Time, Eldrazi Unbound, Heavenly Inferno, Merciless Rage, and so on. Measured with `g7c-decks.mjs`: Azorius Control has 4 creatures and 27 lands, Dimir Control (Q07) has 6 creatures, 28 lands, 17 interaction, and 6 sweepers, and Control Center (AFR) has 16 creatures and 26 lands. The bulk argument (few counters and sweepers in bulk) may still justify more creatures, but it has to stand on bulk supply. Severity: **misleading**.

- **Control.** Claim: the 60-card table says creatures "14 to 20", while the text says real control decks play 22 and proposes a 60% cap. Problem: the section disagrees with itself. 60% of 34 to 35 spell slots allows 20 to 21, the table stops at 20, and the cited figure is 22. Evidence: section 4 table against section 5, bullet 3. Severity: **misleading**.

- **Control.** Claim: card advantage "5 to 8" at 60. Problem: no stated reasoning gives this number, and the guide's own evidence is lower. The plan median for the `control` label is 3 by the live role, which even counts cantrips. Named precons spread from 4 to 11. Evidence: `config/learned/plans.json` sizes.60.plans.control, `role cardAdvantage` quantiles [0.042, 0.088, 0.159] x 35 = 1.5 / 3.1 / 5.6. `g7c-decks.mjs`: net card advantage 11 (Azorius Control), 4 (Dimir Control), 7 (Control Center). Severity: **unsupported**.

- **Control.** Claim: "Counterspells at three mana or more meet no role (207 noncreature cards)." Problem: 207 is the number without the removal role. The scratch script checks only the removal clause. Of the 207, 41 meet another role: 39 `cardAdvantage` (Contradict, Confirm Suspicions, Bone to Ash, Complicate), 1 `ramp`, and 1 `sweeper`. Only 166 meet no role. Cancel itself meets no role, as stated. Evidence: helper JSON for `--tag counterspell` (`data/review/scratch/g7c-counter.json`) with Un-sets removed: noncreature 420, mana value 3+ 231, no removal role 207, of those with another role 41, no role at all 166. Severity: **wrong**.

- **Control.** Claim: false friend "Life with no card" (Healing Salve), and the hint "Exclude ... cheap interaction that is `lifegain` without `removal` (54 cards)". Problem: the 54 are mostly pump tricks with a lifelink or lifegain rider, not life-only cards. Only about 22 have no pump or keyword grant. The exclusion is also redundant, because the control interaction hint never counts `combat-trick`. Evidence: `node data/review/scratch/g7chk/hints.mjs` gives 54 total and 22 without "gets +N", a +1/+1 counter, or a keyword grant (Healing Salve, Healing Grace, Candles' Glow, Awe Strike, Alabaster Potion). Examples among the other 32: Give In to Violence "Target creature gets +2/+2 and gains lifelink until end of turn." and Desperate Lunge "Target creature gets +2/+2 and gains flying until end of turn. You gain 2 life." Severity: **misleading**.

- **Control.** Claim: sweepers "1 to 3 in black, red, white" at 60. Problem: the text's own count puts white (7 common or uncommon) next to blue (8), below green (20). White qualifies only through rares (131 mono-white rare or mythic sweepers), and the text never says so. Evidence: the `g7-gaps.mjs` output, plus a recount by rarity: rare/mythic W 131, U 47, B 125, R 140, G 13. Severity: **misleading**.

- **Midrange and Control.** Missing false friend: bounce counted as removal. Unsummon reads "Return target creature to its owner's hand." It meets `removal` and `cheapInteraction` at strength 7.73, above Murder (7.05). The guide's hints ("Removal: require `removal-creature`", control "Interaction: `removal-creature`") keep it, because `removal-bounce` cards carry `removal-creature`. For Control a bounce spell spends a card to delay one. Evidence: helper on Unsummon. 236 noncreature cards meet the removal role through bounce alone, with no destroy or exile tag. Severity: **missing**.

- **Midrange and Control.** Missing false friend: narrow removal that is not color hate. Plummet reads "Destroy target creature with flying." It meets `removal` and `cheapInteraction` at strength 6.8, and it is not `single-minded-color-hate`, so "require `removal-creature`; drop `single-minded-color-hate`" keeps it. Evidence: helper on Plummet (tags `hate-flying`, `removal-creature`). 105 removal-role cards carry `hate-flying`. Severity: **missing**.

- **Midrange.** Claim: "Circle of Protection: Red ... is the same kind" (as Pyroblast, which "counts as removal and cheap interaction"). Problem: Circle of Protection: Red meets no role and scores 4.05, so the live builder has no reason to pick it. Next to Pyroblast it reads as a live matcher risk, which it is not. Evidence: helper prints `roles: none`, `strength: 4.05`. Severity: **misleading**.

- **Aggro.** Claim: the 60-card table (lands 23 to 24, creatures 20 to 26, curve 6-9 / 11-14 / 8-10 / 2-4 / 0-2, reach 6+). Problem: real aggro decks fall outside it, and the 23-land floor is below what aggro decks play. Mono White Aggro (Challenger Q07) has 25 lands, 30 creatures, curve 15 / 13 / 7 / 0 / 0, and 5 reach by the guide's own reach hint, which misses its token flyers. Mono-Red Aggro (Q05) has 27 creatures and 12 one-drops. The section should say whether the upper ends are limits. Evidence: `g7c-decks.mjs` output. `plans.json` sizes.60.plans.aggro gives lands [24, 25, 25]. Severity: **misleading**.

- **Aggro.** Claim: at 100, "Reach 6+" and "the deck still wants 6 to 8 ramp". Problem: the reach floor is the same as at 60, though the section itself cites 40 life per opponent. Ramp is in the text but not in the table, and the 100 row gives no attacker count. Evidence: section 4 table and the paragraph under it. Severity: **misleading**.

- **Aggro.** Hint: "Reach: `burn-player` without `sweeper`". Problem: it has no mana cap, so it counts Lava Axe, which the same section lists as a false friend. Evidence: `hints.mjs` finds 1,648 matches, 179 of them noncreature at mana value 5 or more (Lava Axe, Aether Charge, Arc Blade). Severity: **misleading**.

- **Aggro.** Hint: "Pump: `combat-trick`, ...". Problem: `combat-trick` also covers removal and life tricks. Disfigure ("Target creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn.") and Healing Salve both carry it, so they would count as pump. `giant-growth` or `enlarge` (Brute Force has both), or a "gets +N" text test, is closer. Evidence: helper tags for Disfigure and Healing Salve. `hints.mjs`: 130 `combat-trick` cards give -N. Severity: **misleading**.

- **Aggro.** Hint: "no `Defender` keyword" and "Exclude: `Defender`". Problem: the matcher schema can test `anyKeywords` but cannot exclude a keyword, and no `defender` tag exists. The guide flags only power as needing code. `excludeTags: ["drawback"]` catches 310 of 311 Defender cards, but it also drops other cards. Evidence: `MatcherClause` in `packages/core/src/config.ts` (anyTags, allTags, excludeTags, anyKeywords, types, anyTypes, excludeTypes, typeLineAny, excludeTypeLine, derived, minMv, maxMv). `hints.mjs`: `tag defender: 0`. Severity: **misleading**.

- **Aggro.** Hint: "Vanillas have empty text and no tags (Savannah Lions)". Problem: two of the guide's own vanillas have tags. A no-tags test misses them, so the test must be empty oracle text. Evidence: helper on Gray Ogre (`tags: cycle-lea-red-escalate`) and Craw Wurm (`tags: meme`). Severity: **wrong**.

- **Midrange.** Hint: "Efficient body: power plus toughness at least twice the mana value at three mana or less." Problem: walls pass. Wall of Wood (0/3 for {G}) scores 3 against a bar of 2. The hint needs a Defender exclusion, which has the same schema limit as above. Evidence: `node -e` count over the pack: 157 Defender creatures at mana value 3 or less pass. Severity: **misleading**.

- **Midrange.** Hints: "Value creature: `virtual-vanilla` or `virtual-french-vanilla` plus ..." and "Top end: ... or an enters effect". Problem: the value hint has no creature test, and five checked cards overstate its precision. "An enters effect" names no tag or text test, so a programmer cannot implement it as written. Evidence: `hints.mjs` finds 663 value matches. 21 are not creatures (Carrion Cruiser, Fell Gravship). 56 creatures have no enters, dies, or leaves trigger (Bloated Toad, Avian Oddity). 149 creatures with "When ... enters, draw/destroy/exile/return" lack both tags (Aven Interrupter, Annex Sentry). Severity: **misleading**.

- **All shapes (Judging a plain deck).** Claim: "If many cards ask for one resource ... and little supplies it, judge it as a theme deck that does not work." Problem: there is no number. On counts alone, the owner's Blue Graveyard deck passes the Midrange row: about 20 creatures, removal that happens to surveil 1, a few draw spells, and big creatures. The value-creature hint even rewards its recursion payoffs (Gravedigger meets it). Without a measurable rule, the shapes guide becomes the escape hatch for exactly the failure the review targets. A usable rule would count cards whose text needs a resource against separate cards that supply it, and fail the deck when the needers pass a floor (say 6 at 60) and the suppliers number under half of them. Evidence: `docs/algo-review.md`, "Why it strings random cards together", and the Midrange section 4 table. Severity: **missing**.

- **All shapes.** Omission: color requirements. The goal's definition of decent includes "color sources", but the sections give land counts only. Nothing covers how many sources a two-color aggro deck needs for one-drops, or how many colored pips at one and two mana are too many. Evidence: `docs/algo-review.md`, "The goal". No mention in any of the three sections. Severity: **missing**.

## Verdict

**needs-fixes.** The card facts and config readings are accurate. Fix the turn-3 odds claim, the 207 count, and the "real control decks" evidence before builders use these numbers. Add the missing false friends (bounce, flyer-only removal) and a measurable failed-theme rule.
