Bulk Brew privacy policy

Applies to the Bulk Brew app for iPhone and iPad and the website. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Bulk Brew has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. Your bulk list, your decks, and your settings stay on your device. Nothing about you is collected, sold, or shared.

What stays on your device

The cards you import, the decks you build or save, and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage (or your browser's storage on the website). They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, deletes them.

Export and backup files are saved only where you choose; in the app, they go to the share sheet and you pick where they go. Importing or restoring reads only the file or text you pick, or a file you open in Bulk Brew from another app. The clipboard is used only when you press Copy.

Network requests

Bulk Brew makes only these requests, and none of them carries your lists or decks:

  • Card images from Scryfall (scryfall.com), when card art is shown. When an imported card names a particular set, the image for that printing is looked up through Scryfall's API by the card's name or set and number. Like any web server, Scryfall sees your device's IP address and which images are requested.
  • Card data from the project's own site on GitHub Pages (xbadc0ffe.github.io). The website loads it from there. The app has card data built in, and at most once a day it checks the same site for newer card data, which it downloads in the background and keeps on your device. Nothing about you is sent. GitHub, which hosts the site, sees your device's IP address as any web server does.

Links on the About screen, such as the source code or the credits, open in your browser only when you tap them. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What Bulk Brew does not do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage statistics.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No collection of your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal data.

Children

Bulk Brew collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. The source code is public at github.com/xbadc0ffe/bulk-brew, so anyone can check what the app does.
